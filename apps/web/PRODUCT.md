# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Brazilian residential landlords who rent out their own property: individuals (pessoa física) or a property holding (holding imobiliária), from one rental to dozens, including multi-unit buildings of kitnets. Some self-manage, some leave contracts with an agency (imobiliária), many do both. The authenticated app is designed for landlords at large, not only for the founder's own portfolio (confirmed 2026-10-12). Their recurring job: know whether the rent came in, what needs them, and how the portfolio performs.

## Product Purpose

Kitnets.com is an AI-assisted platform for running rental properties as a business: properties and units, contracts (AI import from PDFs), tenants, agents, agencies, the income ledger (razão de receitas), invoices to tenants (Banco Inter boleto + PIX), energy and water bills, the condominium cost centre, projects (new investments), taxes, market indices and calculators; bookkeeping for holdings is in progress. Success is an owner who decides with numbers, not guesswork.

## Positioning

Every figure comes from the owner's own ledger, contracts and bills, and the platform shows what the agency costs: the accumulated agency fee is shown as the "economia potencial com autogestão", the potential saving of self-management.

## Operating Context

- Agency-managed rent arrives as one monthly deposit per property or unit, net of the agency fee and carrying charges (energy, condominium); self-managed rent arrives through Kitnets invoices. The ledger records each month per unit as previsto (EXPECTED) or confirmado (CONFIRMED).
- Each contract has a rent due day; a month counts as missing only once its due day has passed, never the contract's first month.
- Portuguese (pt-BR) is primary; /en and /es routes exist.
- Owners show the screen to others: an eye toggle blurs identifiers (addresses, CPF/CNPJ, phones) and a dollar toggle blurs every amount.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No mock, sample or estimated figures on dashboards: without real data a figure reads "—" (owner decision, 2026-10-06).
- A figure whose data failed to load reads "—" and says so, never zero.
- Rows of figures belong in the spreadsheet-like tables (filters, sums, column visibility), never in tiles.
- Few big graphic cards rather than grids of label/value pairs (owner, Contratos hub).
- A card is clickable only when the click opens something, and then it shows an arrow.
- Gross rent, agency fee and net rent stay visible as separate figures.
- Rent only: no sale-listing features.
- Not yet available: federal taxes (waiting for the Contábil & Fiscal module) and the Banco Inter statement sync (waiting for API credentials).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Kitnets.com. Voice: plain Portuguese addressed as "você", short sentences that say what a figure is; formulas and help sit behind a "?" rather than in paragraphs.
- The authenticated app keeps its established look across hubs: Geist sans, slate neutrals, emerald brand, one colour per module. Confirmed as binding for the dashboard redesign (2026-10-12): new layout, same look.

## Evidence on Hand

Real figures exist only in the owners' accounts. The product is pre-launch with a waitlist (lista VIP): there are no testimonials, customer counts, case studies or benchmarks, and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Money truth first: what actually came in, from the ledger, against what was due.
2. Never invent a number; an honest dash beats a plausible estimate.
3. Every figure can explain itself: what it is and how it is calculated.
4. Privacy is one click away: every amount and identifier can be hidden.
5. Self-management made visible: the agency's cut is always on the table.
